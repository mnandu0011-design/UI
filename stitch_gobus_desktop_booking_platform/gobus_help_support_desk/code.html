Step-by-step from origin/destination selection to seat reservation

travel_explore

To find buses on your required route, navigate to the Home Page or the Search Buses portal. Enter your Origin City (e.g. Bangalore, Mumbai) and Destination City (e.g. Hyderabad, Pune), select your intended date of travel, and click Search Buses.

Our real-time engine fetches active services across 50,000+ routes, displaying operators, sleeper vs. seater deck configuration, boarding points, punctuality rating, and promotional fares.

Interactive Seat Selection & Sleeper Berths

Berth geometry, ladies quota restrictions, and deck navigation

airline_seat_individual_suite

In multi-tier sleeper luxury coaches:

Lower Deck (Berths L1 - L18)

Offers smoother suspension dynamics, lower lateral motion on mountain curves, and simpler stepping access without high ladder climbing. Recommended for seniors and families.

Upper Deck (Berths U1 - U18)

Elevated panoramic window vantage, enhanced privacy with drawn acoustic curtains, and zero disturbance from floor aisle traffic.

In compliance with national road passenger safety guidelines, any double-sleeper or double-seater pair where one seat is booked by a solo female passenger is automatically marked with our pink-accented Female Protected Quota tag.

The adjoining co-seat can exclusively be reserved by another female passenger. Male passengers attempting to select this seat will receive a validation alert prompting them to select a general berth instead.

Passenger Information & GST Invoicing

Co-passenger registration, government ID mandates, and tax credit

badge

At least one passenger per booking reference must present a valid government-issued photo identification proof during boarding. Accepted documents include:

  • Aadhaar Card (Physical or official DigiLocker electronic copy)
  • Driver's License issued by State Transport Department
  • Indian Passport
  • Voter Identity Card (EPIC)
  • Official Government Employee ID or Student ID from recognized universities

During the checkout step on the Passenger Details form, simply check the “Use GSTIN for Corporate Expense Reimbursement” box. Enter your registered 15-character Goods & Services Tax Identification Number (GSTIN) and Registered Legal Entity Name.

A digitally signed, GST-compliant tax invoice showing 5% or 18% slab breakout will be dispatched to your billing email address simultaneously with the ticket confirmation.

Children below 5 years of age travel free of charge provided they share a berth or seat with an accompanying adult passenger and do not occupy an extra seat. Children aged 5 and above require an individual ticket and berth reservation at standard fares to comply with highway vehicle safety norms.

Completing Bookings & Instant E-Tickets

PNR validation, direct printout, offline PDF storage, and M-Tickets

airplane_ticket

Physical printouts are completely unnecessary for 99.8% of our intercity fleet. All GoBus bus operators are equipped with verified digital hand scanners.

You can simply flash the SMS confirmation, WhatsApp M-Ticket, or PDF barcode on your smartphone screen along with your photo identification when stepping onto the boarding deck.

No need to worry. Simply visit My Bookings on GoBus. Enter your mobile number or email address to instantly view all active, past, and upcoming journeys. You can re-download high-resolution printable PDFs with zero popups or request an instant WhatsApp re-dispatch.

For passenger privacy and operational coordination, vehicle registration plates (e.g. KA-01-EQ-9844) and driver/attendant contact numbers are automatically dispatched via SMS and WhatsApp 60 to 90 minutes before scheduled departure time.

GoBus operates an automated instant reversal integration:

  • UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM): Settled and credited in under 15 minutes directly to your linked virtual payment address (VPA).
  • Net Banking & Debit Cards: Dispatched instantly by our gateway; processing depends on your bank (typically 2 to 4 business hours).

In the rare instance an operator terminates a scheduled trip due to mechanical breakdown or unexpected route conditions, GoBus triggers an immediate 100% full unconditional refund plus an emergency transit compensation credit voucher of ₹250 to book an alternative carrier immediately.
